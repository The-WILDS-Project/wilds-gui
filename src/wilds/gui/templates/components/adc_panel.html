Atmospheric dispersion corrector not installed
The ADC is not installed, so its controls are greyed out. It is a removable module and WILDS works without it; switch it on under Engineering → Configuration → adc when it is fitted.

Two pairs of prisms cancel the atmosphere's dispersion: how much follows the target's zenith distance, which way follows the parallactic angle seen from the slit. Tracking keeps them there while the telescope moves; set them by hand only with tracking off.
